## Photographs — Pexels License

Eleven frames from ten Pexels photographs by one photographer, Charlotte May, from one shoot of one
apartment, downloaded into the project (never hot-linked), then cropped, graded once for the whole
set and exported for their frames. Only the processed frames are in this repository. They are sample
imagery: the apartment is not the (fictional) studio's work, no client or project is attached to it,
and the page labels every photo "Sample" and the gallery "Sample imagery — illustrative design
inspiration". No people appear. Full details and crops: `PHOTOS.md`.
